# PRDS commercial operating kit

September 24, 2026. Templates for owner approval and explicitly scoped broker engagements. No contact has been made and no buyer agreement or revenue is implied. Keep filled forms and customer evidence private, outside this public repository.

## Offer approval checklist

Record buyer/seller identities; approved offer/version; source capability and geography; observation lag; delivered fields; exclusions; input authorization; record/cadence/support caps; intended start; review date; objective defect remedies; price/currency/taxes; payment/cancellation terms; private processing/retention; receipt destination. Reject unapproved exclusivity, unlimited scope, unsupported revenue/contact enrichment, or success-fee substitution.

Proposed sequence: one relevant free evaluation (20–25 candidates only when inventory supports it), then a $300 two-week bounded pilot, then separately agreed $500/month capped continuation. A pilot starts on the agreed date after operational acceptance, not at payment-link creation. No automatic charges or renewals in this experiment.

## Invitation draft — fresh-filing evaluation

“I’m preparing a small, evidence-backed public-record review for commercial-finance brokers. The aim is to filter for your criteria and show why each record deserves a look—not send another undifferentiated list. Would you review a bounded sample and tell me which records are useful and what is missing? The evaluation is limited; any paid pilot would have a separate scope and price.”

Do not send until recipient and context are authorized and a relevant sample can actually be supplied. Do not turn this draft into an automated merchant-outreach campaign.

## Invitation draft — existing-list evaluation

“I’m testing a way to make an existing prospect list easier to work: identify structural problems, match only where the evidence supports it, and make the review priorities explainable. You would keep your current CRM and calling workflow. Would a bounded evaluation against your criteria be useful? We would agree on a private, authorized transfer before any customer data is shared.”

## Sample review agenda

Ask what was actually reviewed, which records were usable, what made records irrelevant, what remained uncertain, how much research was still required, and whether the next bounded delivery merits a paid pilot. Capture comparisons to the buyer's existing workflow only when measured. Do not ask for a vague endorsement as a substitute for usage.

## Feedback record (private, one candidate per delivered revision)

Fields: opaque buyer ID; batch ID; stable candidate ID; delivered revision; received-at; reviewed-at (nullable); status = accepted_for_review | rejected | not_reviewed; reason = relevant | wrong_entity | outside_criteria | stale_for_workflow | duplicate_in_authorized_list | contact_unverified | missing_required_fact | other; optional bounded private note; correction reference. An old review must not silently apply to a later changed revision.

“Accepted” means accepted under the declared review criterion, not an application, funded deal or confirmed intent. Keep conversations/applications/outcomes as distinct buyer-reported events with their attribution limitations.

## Pilot close draft

“Based on the sample review, the proposed next step is a two-week pilot at $300 covering [agreed scope]. It includes [deliverable/cadence/cap], excludes [limitations], and starts [agreed date] once access and delivery are confirmed. There is no automatic renewal. Before payment, we’ll confirm the scope, data handling, objective defect remedies and cancellation terms.”

Bracketed terms are mandatory fields to resolve, not text to send unchanged. Commerce supplies the real approved payment path; no processor enrollment is authorized here.

## Permissioned proof

Record publication permission separately from processing permission. Show the period, denominator, what was reviewed, accepted/rejected/not-reviewed split, measured preparation effort, limitations, and whether payment/continuation actually occurred. Publish aggregates only after privacy review. No testimonial without an actual attributed quote and permission; no extrapolated ROI from compliments.

## Referral request after demonstrated value

“Who else has the same list-quality or prioritization problem? A relevant introduction would be useful; I would offer the same bounded evaluation rather than promise a result we have not established.”

## Partner exploration after a repeatable paid result

Target a CRM implementer, broker-operations consultant, or trainer whose clients have the verified problem. Start with a standard export and a clear division of support. Do not promise integrations, exclusivity, referral compensation, or a reseller licence before economics, rights and terms are accepted.

## Weekly commercial scoreboard

Track qualified buyer inquiries; evaluations delivered; candidates reviewed/accepted/rejected/not-reviewed; paid pilots; paid continuations; actual net receipts; direct resource cost; support/delivery labor and owner time. State period, source, coverage and denominator. Missing evidence is unknown, not zero. A test invoice is not revenue and a downloaded CSV is not a worked lead.

Decision: no inquiries → examine positioning/distribution; no review → examine fit/friction; review but no payment → examine usefulness/scope/price; payment but no renewal → examine recurring value; renewal with poor margin → examine delivery labor/cost/pricing. Do not answer every problem by adding more states.

## Organic publishing queue

Next drafts should use a permissioned, bounded observed batch or answer an actual buyer question. One substantive owner-approved piece per week; shorter adaptations come from that piece. No mass state-page factory or fabricated national market statistics.
